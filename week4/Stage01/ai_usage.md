<h1>Part A - Understand the Problem:AI OFF</h1>


SmartCare needs a small prototype that allows a receptionist to record patient appointments. Each
appointment records patient name, practitioner name and appointment time.

### The system should store:

- Patient name
- Practitioner name
- Appointment date and time
- Appointment status, such as booked, completed or cancelled
- A unique appointment ID
### What functions might be useful?
- Create a new appointment
- View existing appointments
- Edit or reschedule an appointment
- Cancel an appointment
- Search for appointments
- Check practitioner availability
- View a practitioner's schedule
### What could go wrong?
- Two patients could be booked with the same practitioner at the same time.
- Incorrect patient or practitioner details could be entered.
- An invalid appointment date or time could be entered.
- An appointment could be accidentally deleted or changed.
- A cancelled appointment could still appear as active.
- Appointment information could be difficult to find.
### What requirements are unclear?
- Can a patient have multiple appointments?
- What patient information is required besides their name?
- What appointment statuses are required?
